## **1\. Analyze Mobile Micro Interactions**

You just learned about *microinteractions;* although subtle, they play an important role in improving the user experience.

## **Your Turn**\! Considering what you just learned, analyze a mobile application of your choice and find a microinteraction.

1. Briefly describe the microinteraction that you found. Include a screenshot annotating where the microinteraction occurs.

When the user toggles a switch, the app immediately updates which articles are shown in the News tab.

2. Breaking down the microinteraction into its four elements; describe each of the following…  
     
i. Element 1 – Trigger  
        
The user taps the switch next to a category.
        
   ii. Element 2 – Rules

If the switch is OFF, then any article containing this tag is hidden from the News feed.

   iii. Element 3 – Feedback

The News feed updates immediately.

   iv. Element 4 – Loops and Modes  
      
Loop: The interaction can be repeated indefinitely; users can toggle preferences on and off at any time.
