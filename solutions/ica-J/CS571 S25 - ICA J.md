## **2\. Have a Conversation with a Social Agent**

You just learned about psychological theories that place computers as social actors. In this step, you will explore ‘social agents’ you may already be using to understand how designing sociability in chat agents play important roles. 

## **Your Turn**\! Paste the following prompt into [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/) (or any other LLM agent) and see where the conversation takes you.

*Hello GPT\! I'm just looking to chat with you. What's on your mind today?*

1. Describe the personality of the agent. What behaviors did you pick up on?

Friendly and approachable. Answer my question with emoji attached.

2. Do you feel like the agent was consistent in its behavior? Please describe why or why not.

It is consistency. It aims to maintain a polite, respectful tone.

At some point, disagree with the agent. For example, you may say…

*I'd like you to disagree with me. I think X is better than Y. Can you convince me otherwise? I am doing this for a class project, and I need you to take an opposing stance. Please use both positive politeness and negative politeness in your argument.*

**Your Turn**\! Replace X and Y with something (such as JavaScript and Python), have your discussion, and then answer the following questions…

3. What is the face-threatening act that the agent is committing?

The FTA is disagreeing with the user’s opinion, which threatens the user’s positive face — their desire to be liked, approved of, and seen as reasonable.

4. In what ways does the agent use positive and/or negative politeness in its argument?

Positive politeness (supporting the user’s positive face): “I really respect your perspective…”

Negative politeness (respecting the user’s autonomy and minimizing imposition): “I’d gently suggest…”

## **3\. Design Your Agent – Part 1**

## In HW11, BadgerChat Voice, we hope to put more thought and care into the design of our agent. As such, we’re putting a name to a face – meet Bucki\! Bucki is an ENFJ that hopes to guide you along your BadgerChat journey.

## **Your Turn\!**  For each of the following scenarios, suggest 3 potential replies Bucki could give that (a) make use of some politeness strategy(s), where applicable and (b) are internally consistent.  

1. As a welcome message.  
* Hi there! 😊 I’m Bucki — I’m so glad you’re here! Let me know how I can help you get started today.  
* Welcome to BadgerChat! 🦡 I’m Bucki, and I’d love to guide you through anything you need — just ask! 
* Hey! Thanks for joining us — I’m Bucki, your friendly chat companion. What would you like to explore first?

2. When no intent is matched.  
* I’m sorry — I didn’t quite understand that, but I’d really like to help! Could you try rephrasing? 😊  
* Hmm, I’m not sure I caught your meaning — would you mind explaining a bit more?  
* That one slipped past me, but I’m here to help! Could you clarify what you’re looking for?

3. When the user must be logged in to perform a task (such as create/delete a post).  
* You’re so close! 😊 To do that, you’ll just need to log in first — would you like me to help you with that?  
* I’d love to help you with that, but it looks like you’re not logged in yet. Could you log in first?  
* Just a heads-up — that action requires an account. Let me know if you’d like help signing in!

4. When the user forgets a parameter (such as chatroom name).  
* Almost there! 😊 Could you let me know which chatroom you’d like to use?  
* I just need one more detail — which chatroom were you thinking of?  
* No worries! Could you please tell me the chatroom name so I can continue?
